User Personas

Primary Stakeholders
--------------------

* **Security and Identity Engineer**: Builds FIDO2/WebAuthn authenticators and hardware-backed key derivation services requiring persistent TPM 2.0 keys and verifiable NIST P-256 signatures.
* **Linux Infrastructure Architect**: Deploys sovereign, hardware-bound secrets management over ``/dev/tpmrm0`` on Fedora Linux and Alpine containers without heavy C middleware.
* **Autonomous AI Coding Agent**: Relies on strict type annotations, small modular files under 200 lines, and deterministic virtual TPM simulation to verify integrations rapidly.
